under-number fontup

With 'the under-number font' (better known as nasin nanpa), you can see sitelen pona on your computer!

What is it?

At first, all it's glyphs were wack. But with the help of many friends, I've worked hard toward the goal of creating an accessible and featureful font for sitelen pona!

What has used it?
  • From jan Sonja, su: illustrated stories in glyphs and the Persian version of "Toki Pona: The Language of Good"
  • From jan Majeka, translations of "Maya's sweet 16" , lipu "Maya's Conversatie" , and lipu "Mayamail"
  • From jan Ke Tami, "mi pana e lipu"
  • From Ryan Starkey, "The Etymology of Every Toki Pona Word"
  • From daph, "categorised toki pona cheat sheet"
  • ma Seka, a video site for content in Toki Pona
  • And many more things that I have forgotten the links to.
