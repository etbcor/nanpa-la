At the core was a hard object. The hard mass was very energetic and very hot. On the surface of the hard mass, there were many small energy bugs. They consumed energy and heat from the inner rock. Around the bugs, there was so much air that was like goo, and it was a very dense atmosphere. When the bugs wished to move, this used up energy because of the viscous air. The way of the bugs was as such: Be born. Consume the energy from the rock. Create two bugs. Keep consuming energy. Die. Ascend.
